Learn how SRv6's core principles—application-driven programmability, static source-routing for microsecond-level failure recovery, and deterministic path-pinned performance measurement—enabled MRC's success. Looking forward, SRv6's unified data plane positions it as a foundational technology for next-generation AI supercomputers.
