At the Cisco Live 2025 event in Amsterdam, several sessions explored various SRv6 uSID topics. Discover the free collection of on-demand sessions at the [Cisco Live On-Demand Library](https://www.ciscolive.com/on-demand/on-demand-library.html).

**Cees de Gruijter and Michiel Koolen of [Rijkswaterstaat NL](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/)** presented their SRv6 deployment testimonial, highlighting their experiences, motivations, and the benefits they have realised by fully embracing Segment Routing technology. Their presentation included their end-to-end implementation of SRv6 uSID.

Rijkswaterstaat is a public sector organization in the Netherlands, responsible for the design, construction, management and maintenance of the country’s primary infrastructure facilities.
