On September 23-24 2025, over 50 operators gathered in Nice, France, for the latest edition of the SRv6 uSID Roadshow, where the community came together to share progress and insights on SRv6 uSID deployments. This event featured the latest updates on AI Backend, DCI, and WAN architectures, with a brief look at emerging CNI/federal use cases as well. Attendees included representatives from Oracle Cloud, Nebius, Netflix, Deutsche Telekom, BWI, NATO, Swisscom, Colt, Free/Iliad, IIJ Japan, KPN, Orange, and many others.

In addition to the excellent Cisco internal briefings, participants benefited from a rich lineup of operator-driven presentations.
