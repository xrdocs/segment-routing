The rise of generative and agentic AI is set to massively increase network traffic, exposing critical limitations in existing IP transport infrastructure like high latency and siloed operations. To meet these demands, networks must undergo significant architectural evolution, incorporating capacity upgrades, IP/optical convergence, and flexible edge computing. Key technologies like Segment Routing for IPv6 (SRv6) for advanced traffic engineering and service-level agreement (SLA) assurance are crucial for building the scalable, efficient, and programmable networks required for the AI era.

**Programmable networking with Segment Routing (SRv6) and automation to simplify traffic engineering**:

{: .indent}
SRv6 is the best choice for traffic engineering. It can cost-effectively support the robust service-level agreements (SLAs) required for mobile xHaul, enterprise, and residential applications. SRv6-based end-to-end network slicing provides the most flexible and scalable solution in the AI era. Paired with Flex Algo, it allows service providers to create differentiated SLAs, including low-latency services. It is a powerful approach that helps to manage network resources and prioritize VIP users or mission-critical services traffic, avoiding congested links.  Real-time AI inferencing in fulfilling agreed-upon SLAs will enable service providers to monetize their network resources with guaranteed SLAs for various service offerings, such as VIP users, integrated security, and mission-critical assurance.
