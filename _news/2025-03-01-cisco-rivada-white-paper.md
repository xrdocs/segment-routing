In collaboration with Cisco, Rivada is ready to converge the Outernet with traditional terrestrial networks, building an Agile Service Network leveraging SRv6. The solution will be able to provide end to end network slicing and service assurance across non-terrestrial and terrestrial networks.

    Rivada’s Outernet based meshed LEO satellite architecture, leveraging SRv6, offers significant advantages for last mile backhaul connectivity. Read the whitepaper jointly published by Rivada and Cisco.

In collaboration with Cisco, Rivada is ready to converge the Outernet with traditional terrestrial networks. Cisco provides high performance routing using IOSXR based NCS540, NCS55/5700 and 8000 product line, to build agile service network (ASN) with rich SRv6/EVPN feature set, advanced QoS, robust Security and AI enabled automation. Built with standardized API, the solution will be able to provide end to end network slicing and service assurance across non-terrestrial and terrestrial networks.
