In March 2024, [Netdev 0x19 (netdevconf.info)](https://netdevconf.info/0x19/index.html), the Linux Netdev Conference, hosted an [SRv6 Workshop, “SRv6 in Linux Kernel, FRR and eBPF: review the current status and plan the future evolution” (netdevconf.info)](https://netdevconf.info/0x19/sessions/bof/srv6-in-linux-kernel-frr-and-ebpf-review-the-current-status-and-plan-the-future-evolution.html), exploring the evolution of SRv6 in the open-source ecosystem, including Linux Kernel, SONiC, FRRouting(FRR), and eBPF.

This workshop is a collaborative effort between the Cisco SRv6 team and the research team at University of Rome Tor Vergata, led by Prof. Stefano Salsano.

Check out the comprehensive discussion on the robust support of SRv6 in the open-source ecosystem.
