At **[@Scale: Networking 2026](https://atscaleconference.com/events/networking-2026/)** (25 August 2026, Santa Clara), **Alexey Gorovoy** of Nebius presented “Building scalable AI cloud interconnects with SRv6.”

Large-scale customer interconnects demand predictable bandwidth, integrated network services, and consistent operation across data-center and WAN domains. Nebius uses SRv6 as the architectural building block that bridges the virtual cloud environment with the physical network edge.

The session shows how this model delivers end-to-end VPN and traffic-engineering services without protocol translation between DC and WAN, and shares Nebius’ latest implementation experience operating that fabric for GPU-cloud customers.
