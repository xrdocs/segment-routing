At Cisco Connect LatAm 2026, [Telecom District](https://en.telecomdistrict.com/) is being featured as the most innovative success case for its own native SRv6 backbone, which is currently running in live production.

Telecom District completely skipped traditional MPLS to build a 100% native SRv6 backbone from day zero. Today, that choice serves as the company's greatest operational advantage. By eliminating legacy baggage and pairing SRv6 with EVPN, connecting any two endpoints across its network takes just six lines of configuration. There are no complex tunnels to build and no label stacks to distribute—just extreme, automated simplicity.

They didn't just build a network to turn it on; they built an architecture designed to be actively directed for the next 10 years.
